Limitations

  • Pre-trend, narrow but real. Event-time -6 in the Callaway-Sant'Anna event study traces to a single group-time cell (Maryland/Michigan's 2024 cohort vs. controls in 2018) — a genuine, unexplained pre-existing difference, not a broad parallel-trends violation across the panel. See the event study section above.
  • Heterogeneity by jurisdiction size is unidentifiable with the current cohort coverage. The two corrected estimators disagree because the small-jurisdiction subsample is missing two of four treatment cohorts entirely, and the large-jurisdiction subsample's own cohort-level effects flip sign across periods — not because either estimator is wrong.
  • Illinois and Massachusetts have confirmed cure rights but a genuinely unknown adoption year after 8+ and 5+ independent research attempts — coded as pre-2016 by inference, treated as censored/unknown in the design, not a confirmed date.
  • Ohio and Montana are the two weakest-sourced rows in the 51-state treatment coding — not known to be wrong, just less independently corroborated than every other row.
  • Pennsylvania (no statewide law; county-optional cure) is coded untreated at the state level, a documented approximation, not a discovered fact. North Carolina's cure process came from litigation, not statute, and is excluded from the primary treatment variable by definition.
  • ACS demographic controls were deliberately deferred (no Census API key obtained); jurisdiction fixed effects absorb time-invariant covariates regardless.
  • The underlying treatment coding will go stale. 400+ election-related bills were enacted in the first seven months of 2026 alone — this analysis reflects the 2016-2024 panel and coding as validated through several rounds of review, not a live-updating policy tracker.
  • County choropleth coverage: 7 states (WI and the New England states) report EAVS data at the town/municipality level, not county — shown gray on the map above, not mapped incorrectly or silently omitted.

Full methodology, validation history, and data pipeline: see the project repository's PROJECT_PLAN.md (living plan and findings) and HANDOFF.md (orientation for a new reader). No causal claim is made beyond what this design supports.
